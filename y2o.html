Client Report

Youth2Opportunities (Y2O)

Target Users

Who Are We Building For?

Primary Audience

Young individuals aged 12-22 including school students, high-schoolers, college students, and early learners who are exploring career paths and seeking opportunities to build experience.

User Type

The platform is designed for both mobile and desktop users. However, desktop usage is prioritized initially, as users may need a larger screen for activities such as building profiles, showcasing projects, learning content, and applying for internships.

Easy to Use

Can navigate between pages quickly. No confusion.

Safety for Minors

Special attention to data privacy (considerations of protecting phone numbers, personal email addresses, etc), controlled visibility of personal information, and parental consent for restricted actions.

Design Preferences

Look and Feel

Do

  • Clean, modern, and minimalistic layout
  • Clear typography with readable fonts
  • Fresh, professional color scheme appealing to youth
  • Responsive design for desktop and mobile
  • Subtle micro-interactions and hover effects

Avoid

  • Heavy animations or auto-playing videos
  • Flashy or distracting visual effects
  • Complex layouts that reduce usability
  • Elements that slow down page load times
  • Cluttered or overwhelming pages
